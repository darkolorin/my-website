Since it’s Sunday, there’s time to do some graphomania. A bit of analytics on consumer social.
One of the most important metrics is the so-called hourly pattern. Basically any b2c has a time when the audience is most active. And it’s important to keep an eye on which hours which cohorts show up. I’d even say DAU isn’t that important — what matters is this условный “online.” We constantly track the hourly online and try to grow it.
Usually it’s in GA and other analytics (you can do it off logs).
