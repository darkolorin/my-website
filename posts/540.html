By the way, for those who like podcasts and that kind of stuff
https://www.youtube.com/@allin
They cover events pretty well. The most interesting thing I’ve learned from there so far is that Chamath
https://x.com/chamath?s=21
invested in 23 funds and the biggest returns came from funds sized 10–70M, which he says are small, and he thinks that’s where all the money gets made. There’s some confirmation of that in history too: one of the most successful funds, Benchmark, in the early 2010s was exactly “small.”
In the picture, returns from funds: a 100M fund returned 43x and a 220M one returned 12x (which, in his words, is already a mid-sized fund).
You can read about the metrics
https://www.crystalfunds.com/insights/tvpi-vs-dpi-the-measures-that-matter-for-private-equity-investors

инвестировал в 23 фонда и самые большие возвраты были от фондов размером 10-70M, которые по его словам являются небольшими и он считает что все бабки делаются там. Отчасти этому есть подтверждение в истории, один из самых успешных фондов Benchmark в начале 2010х как раз был «небольшой».
