I’ve got one question for all these labs and companies that are pumping the AI hype up to the heavens. Why do people leave these top omega badass teams — and not just people, but leaders? Like, if you see that your so-called lab is on the brink of a breakthrough and conquering the whole world. And you bounce to go build your own startup around post-train. WTF?

I mean, sure, maybe some folks don’t like the people and the vibe. But there’s money and opportunities coming out of their ears in there. Layoffs? Lol. Doubt it. But maybe it’s about equity.
