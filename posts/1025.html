Dear friends! We’ve got a small bot inside the team, we built it for internal purposes. And then we decided to also throw it out to the public and for free (hope we don’t burn through all our credits).

Why we made it. In our team people constantly drop all kinds of links into chats, like a lot, you know, to the point where I just won’t manage to read all those articles, papers, YouTube vids. So we decided to make a tool so we don’t miss stuff. For starters we’re doing it for Telegram and its little chat rooms.

What’s the point:
- the bot in a chat can collect all links and send you a summary to your DMs at a time you set
- the bot doesn’t need admin rights
- the bot can summarize videos (thanks Gemini API) from YouTube
- hopefully it holds up under load :)
-
killer feature: someone dropped a link in the chat about something a couple months ago, and you only remember the keywords — feed those to the bot in the search command and it finds it even if you don’t remember the exact title!
