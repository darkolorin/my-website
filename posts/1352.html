Prove me wrong. Seems like in a normal world the harness and the model always come as a bundle. RL gets slapped on the harness itself too. I’ve tried all that Muse, Wonder, Google Carbon, etc., and even on those pretty damn solid models the aftertaste is still off. And what is there to say about all those attempts to roll a harness without a model.

tldr: a harness detached from the model is this SaaS approach from the past. And I think, globally, it has no future.
