A quick bench for valuing a company:
Annual revenue x 10 x YoY growth = how VCs would value it
Annual profit x 5 + assets - debts = how independent appraisers would value it (409a)
Annual profit x 10 + assets - debts = how PE would value it
Annual profit x 30 = how the public would value it (if the stock were fully liquid)
This is just a rough sketch and obviously depends on the industry and the market (geo). But it does a decent job of forming a ballpark understanding.
