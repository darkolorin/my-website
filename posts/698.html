Everyone gets so hyped about mentoring, helping, searching for meaning, being a teacher. Well yeah, in general.
How does that saying go —
*
it’s not like hauling sacks.
I get hyped too, that’s why I teach students when there’s a chance. And I treat it definitely not like a way to make money. Wonder how many of those guys are ready to help startups for free and without equity. Rhetorical question, obviously.
