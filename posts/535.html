About PMs. A big discussion blew up on Twitter about what the core skill for a product manager is. And surprisingly I liked how a lot of people consolidated around what, to me as well, seems like the most important long-game skill: storytelling.
A few points:
- this doesn’t mean the other skills aren’t important
- storytelling is about the long haul, it helps you explain what exactly you came up with, and it’ll always help
- caught myself thinking it’s basically part of selling (maybe selling really is the most necessary)
