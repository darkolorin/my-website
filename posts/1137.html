An interesting report dropped from the OpenAI team about how people use ChatGPT and what kinds of prompts, the audience, and so on.

In short:
- it’s growing in developing countries with low GDP per capita this year compared to the previous one
- the charts in the report are, as usual, a mess (don’t know why)
- three types of prompts: asking, doing, expressing. What do you think the shares are?
Link to the study (https://www.nber.org/system/files/working_papers/w34255/w34255.pdf)
