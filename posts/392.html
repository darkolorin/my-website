Recently I had the honor of talking to Emmet Shear, aka the co-founder of Twitch and later its CEO already under Amazon. We were discussing the following question: what do you think needs fixing, or what’s more broken.

  • Money is being invested into shitty projects and we need to stop letting it get put there
  • There aren’t enough good projects
  • Everything is fine, there are plenty of projects and the money’s going where it should
