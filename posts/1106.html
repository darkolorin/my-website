a funny study. (https://metr.org/Early_2025_AI_Experienced_OS_Devs_Study.pdf)
Like, does AI actually speed up the dev process. Turned out everyone thinks about a 20% speedup, but it ended up slowing things down by 19%. Reasons, as I see it, are plenty, starting with novelty, painful maintenance, the model constantly changing, a pile of new tools, and so on. But the fact itself is worth a look to read up on.
