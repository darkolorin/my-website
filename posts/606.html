I’m watching this whole constellation of posts about hiring.

2024-02-20 · Original on Telegram

Home | Projects | Essays | RSS | Telegram (RU)

I’m watching this whole constellation of posts about hiring. Like, some people have literally “thousands of resumes,” and some can’t find two programmers (the word programmer hurts my ears, for me it’s engineer already).

And yeah, it’d be fine if these respected gentlemen understood the reason, but they write and ask how can this be. Well it’s actually pretty simple. We’re talking startups.

I’ve never once had problems with hiring. I act according to a
secret
methodology:
1) I only hire from my network, or via a referral
2) usually people go to work for other people (especially the cooler ones who’ve already done everything, or наоборот the younger ones who haven’t done anything big with anyone yet) and globally they want to learn from the CEO / or like a VP and so on. People came in crowds to work with the emperor (Mikhail Parakhin, ex-CTO of Yandex and current head of Ads & Portal at Microsoft), and of course crowds left too. But the effect is clear.
3) everyone I’ve seen prefers b2c over b2b etc. It’s just boring to build admin panels for the hundredth time and solve “interesting tasks for our clients” and “make this
data sync pipeline between enterprises
make the world a better place.”
4) the leader’s charisma means a lot
5) HR at a startup level == a curse

У меня ни разу не было проблем с наймом. Действую по
секретной
методике:
1) нанимаю только из своего нетворка, либо по реферу
