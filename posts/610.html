The power of the community. Probably the biggest one right now.
https://www.cnbc.com/2024/02/22/reddit-files-to-list-ipo-on-nyse-under-the-ticker-rddt.html
Reddit is going IPO. What’s interesting:
- yeah yeah users with high karma apparently can take part
- when calculating the ad market, Russia and China are straight-up removed in plain text
- they’re planning to launch what looks like their own Avito (great move)
- they want, as I understand it, to keep ~10% of revenue in deals for licensing their content for whatever, including LLMs & Search. Google, as I understand it, is the first client for $60M a year.
