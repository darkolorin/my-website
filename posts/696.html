Let’s talk about the hyped-up (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TitZV6k8zfA) release in certain circles, AI Pin by Humane (https://humane.com/aipin). Besides the fact that the company name is kinda straight-up disgusting to me, I’m surprised how people who worked in corporations have absolutely zero understanding of balance between product and features. Like, the guys announce just a ton of functions, create ridiculously huge expectations, crank marketing to the max (Apple-style, “inspiration” and all that). But it turned out all of this is basically a byproduct of having 100M USD onboard from investors.

You can assemble a cool device, make the casing nice and all that. But actually mastering the product—turned out they couldn’t. And can we stop spinning this idea that the first iPhone was shit too, because it wasn’t. Sometimes you need to admit you’re making some garbage and take all the criticism.

If I were them I’d make just one feature and make it really damn good—especially since it was there from the start—and that’s Live Translation. Because that’s actually convenient, anyone who travels knows, especially in Asia. Everything else is just some incomprehensible crap to me, where any $250 phone does it faster and better (and this pin costs $700, btw). And the price bites so hard it means they’re targeting an adult customer, not Gen Z with pocket money.

And yeah, the typical launch mistake is in the screenshot.
