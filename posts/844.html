Quick tip sparked by a recent call. A few tips for cold messages/emails:
- Keep it to 280 characters
- Avoid extra pleasantries at the start because they take up a lot of space
- Phrase a specific ask
- Make the message relevant to the recipient (turns out you have to figure out who the addressee is)
- Avoid excessive bragging because it takes up a lot of space haha
- Don’t push from the get-go​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​ (gimme 200k pleeease)
