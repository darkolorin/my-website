Yesterday GPT 4.5 dropped. I’ll throw in my two cents—my own thoughts or not my own (it’s hard to be original here).

Basically, they designed the current approach to AGI and it’ll never get there. So what do we have. The new version is, in fact, 10% better, but it looks like it cost about a yard of bucks (1B USD!) to train, judging by API pricing. We’re seeing logarithmic scaling: every 10x in spend gives only ~10–15% improvement. A GPT-7 that would surpass GPT-4 the same way GPT-4 surpassed GPT-2 would cost $100 trillion—the entire world GDP.

Sure, there are all sorts of ideas about test-time compute, like “see, here’s how we can make it smarter,” but in that case for every new token it would have to think for days. Which looks, at the very least, unusable in real life for real tasks (easier to just hire a human). And the cost is insane.

And what am I getting at. At the fact that we’ve actually felt out a wall in the progress of “Intelligence” of big models and the transformer’s capabilities. Now it’s left to figure out what architecture handles prediction tasks and so on better than “attention is all you need.”

What do you think?
