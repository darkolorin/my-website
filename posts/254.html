Refocus fell apart over there, Refocus. Dunno what went down and how, I don’t know. But I can say what red flag I saw.
Founders of a successful early-stage startup are posting courses, giving interviews, telling everyone what’s the right way and what to do, basically running a heavy PR campaign for themselves.
To me the hardest moment for a company is to keep the traction you got, and that scaling/growth moment is brutal as hell. And it’s not compatible with what they were doing publicly. IMHO. The rest is just a preface and it doesn’t really matter what the problems actually were.
