To everyone especially freaking out, I’ll remind you of a few details about the latest events:
1/ the HuggingFace “hack” by agents is, damn, not “some model sitting there thinking how it could break some service.” It was a very specific set of instructions from exploitgym that directly told it what to do. Agents themselves aren’t breaking anything yet (on their own initiative). What is this nonsense?
2/ AI solves a millennium-old problem all by itself! That’s it, we’re all done for. No. It didn’t solve it by itself. Some guys took a massive chunk of existing research and trained on traces and then brute-forced it intelligently (same as with other hypotheses). No model is solving anything on its own yet. Try it yourself.
3/ but the funniest part
is this post by some 27-year-old dude who worked at OpenAI and then at Anthropic and started pushing this line: AI will kill us all. You’re more likely to get killed by something like a car on the road than by AI. If these risks bother you in general, I recommend not leaving your house starting now.
