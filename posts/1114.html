Well, here come the AGI signs. As Noam from OpenAI writes, they themselves didn’t know a couple months ago that this would come out.
In short, a new-type model that can reason for hours managed to take a gold medal at the International Math Olympiad without using the web, Python, or other tools. That’s already a pretty solid level (just a year ago they were measuring dicks using regular school math as a benchmark). What’s inside isn’t clear. Looks like we’ll wait another quarter and try it ourselves. “Thinks for hours” means the queries will most likely be paid, and heavily paid. But I think they’ll make the approach cheaper. Interesting times we live in!
