Since my channel is slowly growing, and mostly thanks to more collabs with folders. This time it’s a folder from the folks in the r founders community. (https://t.me/addlist/72XGG5CmczlmODM6)
It’s mostly about specific people’s experiences, so take a look, maybe someone will click with you.
Sneak peak:
- Misha Lyapin has an interesting take on Africa; I’ve literally never done anything for that region, and you can see the cultural dynamics there especially clearly
- proventure is the classic from Denis Efremov, who’s been doing investments for ages
- and in general I recommend the job board :)

Special thanks to the community lead Ekaterina Kurasheva (by the way she also has a channel (https://t.me/katyagrows) if anyone’s interested).

Feels like the audience-swap format works up to a certain scale. We made a lot of communities in Discord and now in Telegram. And it seems like every community, while it’s narrow topic-wise, has a ceiling. And then it slides into generic topics: like how to make money or how not to fall into depression. Which is obviously too general for me personally.

Who’s got their own channel? Who wants a collab?

P.S. Don’t know about you. I spend 5–7 minutes a day on the channel, sometimes less. Just because I keep writing what I think. Probably that’s also why it doesn’t grow faster.
