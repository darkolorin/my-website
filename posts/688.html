Meanwhile Pavel Durov is telling us that Telegram’s DAU/MAU is ~1/2, which is an insanely high metric. And combined with the fact that it’s a messenger, retention should be through the roof too. In short, a grandiose result. Waiting for it to turn into a business.
Similar numbers were seen across all successful consumer (social) companies, and their IPOs were very strong as well. Almost everyone went with an ad model (the exception is probably WeChat, which started building a superapp, but the explanation is more in its locality).
So: DSP, auction, discover, etc. Or we’ll be watching something new. Right now the movement is closer to a Snap/Discord strategy. And their future as a business looks unstable to me.
Would be nice, of course, to see daily time spent per user. Then we’d immediately know the ad potential (and possibly the need to build a feed like in that same WeChat).
