On the importance of distribution:
Ben Silbermann (founder of Pinterest) got his first initial users by going to the Palo Alto Apple Store and changing the Safari homepage to Pinterest on as many iMacs as he could until they kicked him out.
He also shoved paper flyers under doors, by the way.
