For those still interested in the cost of training a model, Anthropic’s CEO dropped an essay with some politics mixed in (and (https://darioamodei.com/on-deepseek-and-export-controls) here AI already looks like an arms race).

One interesting point he emphasized: models that “reason” are always based on a base model and its quality. Meaning all this “reasoning” is basically a consequence of whatever you managed to get earlier as the base (the base is just trained to spit out an answer to a question, like a very smart autocomplete/sentence-finisher, as I like to put it). He also said their cutting-edge model was made for roughly a few dozen million (I’m thinking 25–30).

Anyway, I recommend reading it if you want to understand the AI market. Because this tech will be everywhere, in any business, sooner or later.
