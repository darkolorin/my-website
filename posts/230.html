That’s roughly how I think about a lot of those showcase stories from the market. You can’t learn anything from OpenAI or, like, BeReal. Basically a lot of companies that, explosively or diligently, still took off can’t bring you anything. Their stories are unique, and sometimes even deadly, because in your conditions it won’t repeat.
