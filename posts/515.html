God, I’m so damn sick of these Product Hunt launches that are juiced up via mailings / “ask a friend” / “buy upvotes”.
Like yeah, I get it. But when you open a product and there are 50 comments and all of them are Russian-speaking by origin. What’s the calculation? Like other people will come in and go “wow so cool”.
I’ll be the marketing-backward dude for a bit.
- yeah I believe a cool product takes off on its own, without a hundred upvotes and mailings and begging and prayers to vote
- yeah I believe Product Hunt is a useless platform populated by Russians for like 30–40% for sure
- yeah I believe it’s better to go to Reddit and get honest feedback there, if there’s even anything to get feedback on
- yeah I believe you don’t need any courses, tactics, hacks for Product Hunt
- and yeah I believe the platform is overrated, same as LinkedIn and its fine-tuning
