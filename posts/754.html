Google, predictably, rolled out a bunch of new stuff, but I think commercially they’re ahead in terms of understanding how not to lose money in all this.

AI Overviews. When they hand off part of the queries straight to the little model, right in search. Curious where ads end up in that case, and how much the guys lose. They’re shipping it in the US as early as this week — I’ll be watching the report. But I’m sure they’ve crunched everything and most likely they’ll be leading it into YouTube where ads are growing nicely.

Other than that: a huge window size, a “competitor” to video generation — kinda meh, a bunch of doodads with visual search.
