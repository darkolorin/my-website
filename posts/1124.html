Before any startup/manager/enthusiast goes off building their own agent to “take over the world”, I’d recommend vibe-coding something from scratch to the finish that you’re not ashamed to show someone other than yourself — at least a couple, three features.

P.S. sobers you up real fast and gives you a sense of what an LLM actually is
