A very interesting article (https://www.fxguide.com/fxfeatured/actually-using-sora/) about the experience of working with Sora from OpenAI, the thing that generates video based on your prompt. All the problems are right there, in plain sight. And that’s exactly why all of this is still in deep alpha and I’m afraid it won’t reach normal video production anytime soon. But as a tool for sketches or quick clips, I think it’ll be enough pretty soon. By the way, right now it generates at most 720p, but it’s still slow, so testers do 480p. As for consistency, I’ve been talking about that for a long time. Here it’ll be critically important.
