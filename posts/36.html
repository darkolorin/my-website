Tech right now is like working at the plants and factories of the early 20th century. Like, literally one-to-one, the whole thing is playing out.
- a crisis and layoffs (automation + overhiring)
- assembly-line-ification (AI Tools)
- education will now teach programming or at least understanding what it is everywhere (writing basic prompts or using AI Tools — read: screwdrivers/hammers as shop class)
- the value of a manager and a lead, with an understanding of goals and approaches, of creativity, is going up
- there’s a fuckton of money in the system and it’ll be enough to give birth, against that backdrop, to a new industrialization (most likely biotech)
