It’s kinda interesting that the consumer social company Snap is going into commerce
https://ares.snap.com/
And not their own, but specifically partner-based, and they’re playing it as a technology. It’s hard to say with confidence that things are all bad on the ads side. But it says a lot anyway.
I think if you managed to build something in your product that looks like an ad platform and it works, then right now that’s the most promising startup in the world (yeah yeah not all these AI things on one idea and an API). Because over the last few years only TikTok and Amazon are showing revenue growth from their ads. Waiting for new b2c with an ads model, since attribution and all that is getting more and more complicated.
