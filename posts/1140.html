Ladies and gentlemen, we’re putting together a demo app for MacOS, and then all the features of our engine will start showing up in there. For now it’s in alpha mode, but I’ll be happy if you give feedback and actually try using it.
It works pretty simply: download the .pkg and then, with no logins and other crap, you just download yourself a little model to try. I’m actively taking feature/bug requests. For the best bug and the best feature request, you get a gift from me.
Maybe the app’s code will be Open Source soon.
