I get the urge every day to write a post like:

  • at 18 I’d be nobody
  • at 20 I ditched everything and went to work for some uncle (won’t say for who)
  • at 22 everyone turned me down when I was job hunting (won’t say what kind)
  • at 25 I raised my first investments but everything crashed (won’t say how much from whom)
  • at 27 I realized what I needed to do, but I got betrayed (won’t say by who)
  • at 29 I still haven’t given up and I sleep on a mattress and chase the dream
  • at 30 I built three companies valued at $200M (my friends, won’t say who, helped out)
  • and now a closed stream for … subscribe and then you’ll get access for free

But yeah, somehow the inspiration for a post like that hasn’t shown up yet.
