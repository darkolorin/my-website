It seems to me people consider “hard” the stuff that rarely works out (which is basically the same as high competition). Like, for example, building a startup, doing nothing and making a ton of cash, and so on. Even though personally I don’t see anything hard about building a startup, it’s work, you choose it, and it has its own нюансы.

And when someone pulled it off without suffering and whining — then it’s luck, a freebie, friends helped, etc.

P.S. I don’t know anyone who both made it and, in the process, was constantly whining, “finding themselves” and writing posts
