I’m watching closely how Discord’s monetization is getting rolled out in TG.
1) Nitro for users with extra features
2) boosting servers/channels for various features (though right now it’s a weird implementation)

TBD:
3) channels buying boosts themselves
4) channels releasing their own stickers so people join and buy a subscription

Also Discord ran experiments with its own game store (see tonapps and all that) and it didn’t go all that well.
Overall everyone wants their own WeChat.
