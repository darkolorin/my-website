My two cents on the announcement:
- the first hour of the presentation about announcing sidebars, recoloring icon colors, and all sorts of bullshit you can skip, they brought Android over
- liked the gestures in AirPods, nodded yes, shook my head no, been waiting for that forever
- finally my Mac will start accepting push notifications from the phone, and testing will be more convenient

Then AI. Musk got toasted by a privacy-first company :) good. But overall I still don’t really get how all of this is going to work quality-wise. What matters here is how much the system will adapt and whether it will at all. Or did we just freeze the weights in memory and that’s it? What am I going to be sending to OpenAI?

It’ll only work on the latest devices. And that’s fair. The company is still a hardware seller and needs to boost sales. That’s why they don’t give a damn about old devices.
