A super important topic in the release write-up (lolkek, not even a “work”) on Sora. If you keep it short, of course they didn’t say jack shit about how and what was done, as usual. BUT there’s this:

Simulating digital worlds. Sora is also able to simulate artificial processes–one example is video games. Sora can simultaneously control the player in Minecraft with a basic policy while also rendering the world and its dynamics in high fidelity. These capabilities can be elicited zero-shot by prompting Sora with captions mentioning “Minecraft.”

This finally brings us closer to real VR and full personalization of games.
