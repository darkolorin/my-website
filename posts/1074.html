Haven’t had the “read it but didn’t fully get it” section in a while. Alright, let’s go.
1/ Ran into a short post (https://dynomight.net/smart/) about AGI, where, IMO, it touches the question “what does it even mean to be smarter than a human and what would that give us.” Because the definition of AGI is blurry—what does “automating discoveries” mean, acceleration? As for me, being afraid of super-intelligence in the current reality isn’t even worth it. Interesting.
2/ thought Roblox is rolling in profit and living the sweet life? I like Matthew’s articles (https://www.matthewball.co/all/roblox2024).
3/ Runa has some entertaining stats (https://runacap.com/ross-index/q1-2025/) on growing open source startups
4/ read Schopenhauer? If not — I recommend it, Einstein really loved his work on the will
5/ gonna fork the open source version of cursor (https://voideditor.com/) haha
6/ Last summer Goldman Sachs put out an interesting analysis (https://www.goldmansachs.com/images/migrated/insights/pages/gs-research/gen-ai--too-much-spend,-too-little-benefit-/TOM_AI%202.0_ForRedaction.pdf) about AI. We’re spending a lot and getting little benefit.
7/ beat a couple levels in the game (https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/level-devil-not-a-troll-game/id6477413486) — “relax”
8/ the most promising option for a theory of everything (or unifying quantum field theory and gravity/GR) is the holographic principle. Lex has a decent explanation (https://youtu.be/1_ibTNDV8aU?si=RGKcA_DVhGuByhQw) from a guest. And in general I recommend reading up on it.
