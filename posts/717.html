Predictably, the folks at Perplexity have figured out how to beat Google. Easy: start selling an enterprise solution. (https://www.perplexity.ai/enterprise/)

Overall, given the amount of stuff the company is doing:
- API
- search? Where they literally say in interviews they’ll win via UX (also building their own search crawler)
- enterprise solution, like “sell your colleagues a search bot so query data doesn’t leak” (corporate search as a business is something new)
- subscriptions
- news
- distribution partnerships for their search

You get this feeling that after raising a ton of money and becoming a unicorn (https://www.perplexity.ai/hub/blog/perplexity-launches-enterprise-pro) they (probably) don’t actually understand how to build a growing business or product.
No focus. And the founder is constantly taking photos with a bunch of cool guys and doing interviews/podcasts. My bet: if they don’t figure out what to do this year as their core, they’re in for a slow death. Yet again I’m convinced that raising a ton of money (https://techcrunch.com/2024/04/23/perplexity-is-raising-250m-at-2-point-5-3b-valuation-ai-search-sources-say/) before PMF (product-market fit) isn’t always the best idea. What do you think?
