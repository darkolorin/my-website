So, here’s a solid list of works I recommend everyone read. Links to them should be, or already are, in pretty much every significant piece by OpenAI etc.

1/
Grice (https://web.stanford.edu/class/psych205/papers/Grice-1975.pdf)
about communication

2/
the magic number 7 (https://labs.la.utexas.edu/gilden/files/2016/04/MagicNumberSeven-Miller1956.pdf)
about information and how we perceive it

3/ not many places have it
link (https://homepages.cwi.nl/~pdg/ftp/mdlintro.pdf)
but getting familiar with Kolmogorov complexity and the MDL principle is basic

4/ recently there was hype that LLMs are the best compressors, so yeah
a review (https://www.greene.sh/compression-and-intelligence/)
