Can’t not write about Pavel. I’m not going to spin up theories, more like underline the important stuff about the internet and products there.
1/ The age of global consumer products is over, and yeah I think B2B too.
2/ When you’re big, it’s impossible to stay neutral. “Big” is from a billion valuation and ~100M monthly users, somewhere around there.
3/ The internet will be regulated by states just as strictly as regular offline life, maybe even harsher, since it’s formally easier to keep tabs.
4/ That kind of split will create a huge number of opportunities for new products and startups across different markets.
And there are already plenty of theories about Pavel Valeryevich. Not my thing to stir up that muck.
