Man, Sam Altman’s got such a ridiculously good position. Just think about it:

  • you ship super-heavy closed models that need unreal resources (compute)
  • you bend everyone into accepting that training this stuff takes a shitload of servers
  • you go raise money for your magic
  • and then you go “boys, the future money of the world is compute”

Genius. As a seller of startups and technologies, I’d say he’s absolutely top of his era.
