Managed to get into a closed event in Paris where Anthropic’s CEO — Dario Amodei — was speaking. Overall he pretty quickly retold his latest blog post, but dropped an excellent spoiler on what to expect from Anthropic’s new model. I’ll write more later once the event wraps up.

Update: well, so Dario gave a fairly detailed example that a person chooses for themselves when to think through a task and when to answer fast. And he’s very surprised that there are “thinking” models and there are ones that supposedly answer right away. He hinted that in Anthropic’s new model, the model itself will decide whether to think or not. That’s exactly what’s needed. At least someone isn’t doing everything just to ship for the sake of benchmark metrics.
