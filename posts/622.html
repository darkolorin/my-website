Of course, everyone’s probably already read that Elon Musk filed a lawsuit against OpenAI and against Sam in particular, like you’re such a traitor and started cashing in on everything. But I suspect that besides the ephemeral chance of winning (or just dragging it out, for example), one of the ideas might be this: often companies that have a lawsuit filed against them can’t sign serious B2B deals, and it takes a lot more time to come to an agreement. Feels like a tactical move to me. правда, what for? Not sure yet.

P.S. Also people say like that’s how they’ll find out info about GPT-4 or like 5, because the law firm is small. But I still think it’s about slowing down the number of deals. We’ll see.
