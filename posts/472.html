Sam’s in his place. Satya, I think, got a bit sad. And Musk posted some interesting emails that were sent to him.
Why everyone’s staring so hard:
- standard drama, understandable inside the тусовка
- hype around OpenAI and, accordingly, almost the most expensive startup right now
- just to be in the know
But for me it’s more interesting how the fans of YC / the religion of how to “properly” run a startup and what to do, and the worshippers of all this, were in the process saying what a god Sam is. I don’t share that cult. And what’s more, I’m внимательно studying how this тусовка behaves in scandals this big. And it looks like a total mess, which says the approach is kinda bankrupt. But it’s hard to draw conclusions “for now”.
